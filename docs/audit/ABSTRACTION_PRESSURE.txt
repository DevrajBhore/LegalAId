6 propositions cannot be classified mechanically. The question for each is whether

and that is a legal judgement, not a string match.

12 of 17 orphan gate uses withhold a clause that ships UNGATED in another family
(11 distinct clauses). For these the gate is not protecting the reader from anything;
it is producing two different documents from one library for no recorded reason.

  external instrument     5 families   SHA-like: the document's correctness depends on a document it is not
