4 of 4 families collect the side and produce identical legal substance
either way: MASTER_SERVICE_AGREEMENT, NDA, LOAN_AGREEMENT, EMPLOYMENT_CONTRACT.

The document is not wrong. The PRODUCT CLAIM is: the intake asks "Which side are you on?"
and says the answer points the protections at the right side of the table. It does not.
A user who answers Customer and a user who answers Supplier receive the same instrument,
having each been told their answer shaped it.

This is the assumed-side invariant in a new place. A document must never look fully
fact-specific while resting on a side nobody acted on -- and here nobody acted on it even
though the user did confirm it.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY: that opposing sides ought to produce different documents. Plenty
of provisions should be identical whoever asked. The defect is the gap between what the
question promises and what the pipeline does with the answer -- fixable either by routing
the side into authored positions, or by not presenting it as a drafting input.
