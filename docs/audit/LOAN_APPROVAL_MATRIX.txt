========================================================================================================
THE CONTROL
========================================================================================================
  CONTENT:             required=YES obtained=UNKNOWN -> RESOLVED   |   obtained=YES -> RESOLVED
  IDENTICAL. A requirement satisfied by clause presence cannot tell a drafted obligation
  from a discharged one. The document PROMISES drawdown waits for approvals, and the
  promise is what satisfies the requirement.

  EXTERNAL_COHERENCE:  required=YES obtained=UNKNOWN -> UNVERIFIABLE/NOT_ESTABLISHED   |   obtained=YES -> RESOLVED/ESTABLISHED_POSITIVE
  THEY DIFFER. The existing external-coherence shape already separates required from
  obtained, without any new abstraction.

========================================================================================================
THE DIMENSION THAT IS NOT COVERED
========================================================================================================
  genuinely issued        -> RESOLVED/ESTABLISHED_POSITIVE
  'issued' by the Borrower's own compliance team -> RESOLVED/ESTABLISHED_POSITIVE
  IDENTICAL. subject_binding ties a record to THIS TRANSACTION, and there is no way to
  say who is competent to grant the approval. A Loan Registration Number is issued by
  the Reserve Bank through an authorised dealer bank; a record asserting one, about the
  right deal, from anybody at all, is accepted.

  This is NOT the required-vs-obtained gap — that one is already covered. It is a third
  binding: evidence is bound to a transaction and is not bound to an AUTHORITY.

========================================================================================================
THE SAME QUESTION, ASKED OF THE PROPOSITION LAYER
========================================================================================================
  lender_is_nbfc, on a public register        -> EVIDENCE_PRESENT
  lender_is_nbfc, on the lender's own say-so  -> EVIDENCE_INADMISSIBLE
  The proposition layer ALREADY constrains who may establish a proposition -- that is what
  admissible_provenance is. So authority-competence is not a missing capability in the
  architecture. It is a capability present in one mechanism and absent from the other:

      evidencePropositions   binds to a subject AND to an admissible source
      EXTERNAL_COHERENCE     binds to a subject only

  Which is a smaller and more tractable finding than 'third-party consent needs a new
  abstraction', and it is NOT a licence to author a requirement onto the EVIDENCE source
  to demonstrate it. Whether an LRN belongs there is a question about the Loan family's
  real requirements, and this probe authored none.
