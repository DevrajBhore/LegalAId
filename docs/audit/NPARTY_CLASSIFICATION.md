## Reading

Invariant 60's twenty-three clauses were the ones one MSA fixture reached,
not the portfolio. Measured across every family that can admit a third
principal, the classified fraction is **12.5%**.

The number that matters is not how many clauses are unresolved. It is that
**6** of them are unresolved because a legal question was identified
and deliberately left open, and **161** are unresolved because nobody has looked.
Those are different states with different owners, and before this sweep the
repository could not tell them apart at portfolio scale.
